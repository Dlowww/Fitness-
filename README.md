# Workout Log 🔥

A workout app for your phone. It logs lifts, runs, weight, mobility, streaks, badges and PT tests. It works offline at the gym, and all your data stays on your phone.

## 1. Publish it (one time, about 3 minutes)

1. On github.com, open the repository **dlowww/fitness-**.
2. **Make it public:** click **Settings** → scroll to the bottom ("Danger Zone") → **Change visibility** → **Public** → confirm.
   *(Only the app's code becomes visible. Your workout data never leaves your phone.)*
3. **Turn on GitHub Pages:** **Settings** → **Pages** (left menu).
   - Under "Build and deployment", set Source to **Deploy from a branch**.
   - Choose the branch **ccr-ba0ee440-g74173**, folder **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the page. You'll see your address:
   **https://dlowww.github.io/fitness-/**

## 2. Put it on your phone

**iPhone:** open the address above in **Safari** (it must be Safari) → tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

**Android:** open it in **Chrome** → tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

Always open the app from the home-screen icon. It then runs full-screen and works with no signal.

## 3. First time you open it

Enter your Training Maxes. If you don't know them, tap **Calculate** and type a recent set, like 185 × 5. Then tap **LET'S GO**.

## Keep your data safe

- Everything is stored on your phone only. About every two weeks, tap **Settings → Export backup** and save the file to iCloud Drive / Google Drive / email. The app reminds you.
- New phone? Install the app, then **Settings → Import backup**.
- On iPhone, always use the home-screen app. Safari can clear data from websites you haven't visited in a while, but not from home-screen apps.

## Good to know

- **Timer sounds:** keep the phone unlocked during a run. The timer keeps your screen awake. iPhones don't allow vibration from web apps, so you get beeps and a voice ("Run", "Walk") instead.
- **Missed a day?** Nothing breaks. "Next up" waits for you, and the streak survives one missed day (just never miss twice in a row).
- **Logged something wrong?** Progress → History → **Undo** on the latest session.
- **Plan out of sync?** Settings → Schedule → "Adjust plan position".
- **Updates:** after a new version is published, close and reopen the app (sometimes twice) to get it.
